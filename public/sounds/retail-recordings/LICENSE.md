# Retail hardware recordings

These cues are real retail and payment-device recordings sourced from
Freesound and distributed under CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication).
They are intentionally used sparingly: the app is silent by default for
completed sales, terminal payments, scanner feedback, and webshop orders.

| File | Source | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `cash-drawer-receipt.mp3` | [cash drawer and receipt.wav](https://freesound.org/people/kalisemorrison/sounds/202531/) by kalisemorrison | Optional completed in-app cash sale |
| `payment-terminal-approved.mp3` | [Contactloos betalen pinnen pinautomaat payment](https://freesound.org/people/Mega-X-stream/sounds/638835/) by Mega-X-stream | Optional terminal-payment confirmation |
| `scanner-beep.mp3` | [scanner beep.wav](https://freesound.org/people/kalisemorrison/sounds/202530/) by kalisemorrison | Attention and optional scanner/order feedback |

redistribution without attribution. Source pages and licence status were
