# POS software notification cues

The register intentionally uses sound only for events owned by the software:
a failed checkout requiring attention and a genuinely new webshop order.
Payments, barcode scans, keypad input, printers, and cash drawers stay silent in
the app because their physical hardware already provides feedback.

The two OGG files are selected from the hand-crafted, subtle notification-tone
collection by Antti Kaihola:

https://github.com/akx/Notifications/tree/db184d33834a94028b6600d155d312ce7b704e8d/OGG

- `taptap.ogg` — short attention cue (`Taptap.ogg`)
- `polite.ogg` — new webshop order (`Polite.ogg`)

The collection is offered under CC0/Public Domain as documented in its README:

https://github.com/akx/Notifications/blob/db184d33834a94028b6600d155d312ce7b704e8d/README.md

`key-click.mp3` is the 31 ms “Tic Toc Click” by Breviceps, used exclusively
for the cash-payment keypad. It is published under CC0/Public Domain:

https://freesound.org/people/Breviceps/sounds/448081/
